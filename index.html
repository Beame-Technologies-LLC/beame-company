What we do

Beame Technologies LLC develops and operates a mobile marketplace for on-demand photography. Customers book a session from their phone; a nearby vetted photographer travels to them, shoots the session in person, and the finished photos are delivered digitally to the customer.

Our platform handles the full experience end to end — matching and dispatch, live arrival tracking, in-app photo capture and delivery, payments, and support for both customers and the independent photographers who work through the platform.

How it works

  • Book. A customer chooses a location, a session length, and a time — now or scheduled.
  • Match. The request goes to background-checked photographers nearby who are available to work.
  • Shoot. The photographer arrives and photographs the session in person.
  • Deliver. Photos land in the customer's account. Customer privacy is built into the product, not bolted on.

Photographers

Independent photographers use the platform to find paid work near them, on their own schedule, with transparent per-session earnings and no obligation to accept any particular booking. Photographers are screened before they can accept work.
